How deep should you fine-tune, before the model stops looking at the lungs?

We trained three ResNet50 models on chest X-rays under increasing fine-tuning depth, then measured not just accuracy — but where each model was actually looking when it made a call.

The Core Finding

Full fine-tuning wins on accuracy — and loses the lungs.

Going from a frozen backbone to partial fine-tuning improves everything at once. Pushing further to full end-to-end fine-tuning buys a marginal accuracy gain, but Grad-CAM and SHAP both converge on the same story: the model quietly shifts its attention to the image borders — a textbook case of shortcut learning.
